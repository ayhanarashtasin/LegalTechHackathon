Five Doors, One Record: Integrated Digital Legal Aid System (DLAS)

Solution paper · ADLASB Grand Finale · 26 September 2026 · all data fictional

One system, not 23 islands: every citizen door, provider role and technical feature works on one authoritative Application/Case record. AI suggests; authorised humans decide.

1. Architecture and integration map

React/Vite PWA → Express 5 API (MVC) → MongoDB with transactions. Submission issues an Application ID; only a DLAO officer's acceptance creates the Case ID. Facts, consent, safe contact, tasks, documents, referrals, mediation, lawyer work and audit attach to that record. The server enforces role, office and assignment. Optional Groq AI always has a manual fallback.

3. Human safeguards

  • AI proposes, humans decide: AI never sets eligibility, priority, route, lawyer, outcome or provenance.
  • Every override and decision needs a reason and is audited.
  • Scores and alerts open reviews; they never reject, merge or find misconduct.
  • Signatures are not a decree; CLAO certification is a separate authorised step.
  • Demo thresholds await law-team approval.
